How it works

  • Mount the phone safely in the vehicle (or hold it steady) and start recording before you drive.
  • The accelerometer measures vertical bumps; GPS tags where they happen.
  • Readings are batched and uploaded every ~10 s (needs internet; if offline, the last batch is retried).
  • View the combined map at the dashboard.
